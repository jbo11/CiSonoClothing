# Customer Growth Admin Checklist

These items are recommended by the audit but usually live in Shopify admin settings or apps instead of the theme repository.

## Policies

Update the refund policy in Shopify admin:

- Remove unrelated boilerplate for perishable goods, software, CDs/DVDs, and health/personal care items.
- Keep policy language specific to apparel, accessories, sale items, exchanges, and damaged items.
- Add clear customer-friendly sections for return window, item condition, exchange process, refund timing, and contact method.

## Product Content

For the top 20 products by traffic or revenue:

- Add fit notes.
- Add fabric/feel details.
- Add care instructions.
- Add 2-3 styling suggestions.
- Add SEO title and meta description.

## Apps and Automations

Consider enabling:

- Product reviews with photo reviews or verified purchase badges.
- Back-in-stock alerts for sold-out variants.
- Welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase, and review request email flows.

## Suggested Email Offer

Use this newsletter value proposition:

"Get new arrivals, easy outfit ideas, and first access to limited restocks."
